How to import

  1. Preview a drill, then download the JSON.
  2. On your phone: open the file with Tabata Core, or go to Settings → Templates → Import.
  3. The plan appears as an imported template — Duplicate to customize.

In the app you can also use Check for online drills under Templates to sync automatically.
