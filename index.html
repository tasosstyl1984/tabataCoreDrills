How to import

  1. Preview a plan, then download the JSON.
  2. On your phone: open the file with Tabata Core, or go to Settings → Plans → Import.
  3. The plan appears as an imported plan — Duplicate to customize.

In the app you can also use Get online plans under Plans to sync automatically.
