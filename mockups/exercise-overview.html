Exercise overview

Same Barbell Chest Press words, four ways to set the type. Today is what the app and the site do now. The other three only change spacing, weight, and a little color.

1. Today

One blue label, then a block of semibold text. The two sentences look the same, so the eye has nowhere to land.

How to
Barbell Chest Press
Exercise image

Setup

Lie on the bench with your eyes under the bar. Plant your feet, pinch your shoulder blades together, and grip the bar just outside your shoulders.

Action

Lift the bar off the rack, lower it to the middle of your chest with your elbows near your sides, then press it straight up until your arms are straight.

Form check

Keep your hips on the bench and your wrists over your elbows. Do not bounce the bar off your chest or let your elbows flare straight out.
